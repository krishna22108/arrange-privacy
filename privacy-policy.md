# Privacy Policy for ARrange

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at **ARrange** ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

When you use the ARrange mobile application ("App"), you trust us with your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

### Personal Information You Provide to Us
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering for the App. This includes:
*   **Account Credentials:** We collect your email address and password to create and secure your account.

### Data Collected Through App Permissions
To provide the core functionality of ARrange, we request specific device permissions:
*   **Camera Access:** We require camera access to allow you to capture photos of your furniture and to enable Augmented Reality (AR) spatial tracking for measuring and placing furniture in your environment.
*   **Photo Library/Storage Access:** We require access to your photo gallery so you can select existing images of furniture to import into the App.

### User-Generated Content
We collect the data you create within the app to ensure it is synced across your devices:
*   **Images:** Photos of furniture you capture or upload.
*   **Metadata:** Dimensions, notes, category tags, and names you assign to your furniture items.
*   **Spatial Measurements:** Measurements you record using the AR measurement utility.

## 2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We strictly process your information to provide, improve, and administer our App:

*   **On-Device AI Processing (Privacy First):** ARrange features automated AI background removal for your furniture photos. **This AI processing happens entirely on your local device.** Your photos are *never* sent to a third-party AI server for background extraction.
*   **Augmented Reality (ARCore):** We use Google's ARCore to understand your physical space for furniture placement and measurement. AR tracking data is processed locally on your device in real-time.
*   **Cloud Synchronization:** We use a local-first architecture. Your data is saved locally on your device first, and then securely synchronized to our backend cloud database (hosted on Supabase) so you can access your catalog from any device you log into.

## 3. Will Your Information Be Shared with Anyone?

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with the following trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App:

*   **Supabase:** We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure. Supabase securely stores your account credentials, database records (furniture data, measurements), and cloud images. Supabase complies with strict data privacy and security standards. 
*   **Google Play Services:** For Android users, the app relies on Google ARCore and Google Play Services for spatial tracking and app delivery.

We will only disclose your information where we are legally required to do so in order to comply with applicable law, governmental requests, a judicial proceeding, court order, or legal process.

## 4. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. All data synchronized between your device and our backend infrastructure is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS). Your account credentials and data are securely stored in our cloud infrastructure. 

However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 5. Do We Collect Information from Minors?

We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 13 years of age. By using the App, you represent that you are at least 13 or that you are the parent or guardian of such a minor and consent to such minor dependent’s use of the App.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. You can review, change, or terminate your account at any time.

**Account and Data Deletion:**
You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data. To request data deletion, please contact us at **[arrange642@gmail.com]**. Upon request, we will delete your account and all synced user-generated content from our active databases.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Effective Date" and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible.
