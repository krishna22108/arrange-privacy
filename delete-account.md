# Request Account and Data Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your **ARrange** account and all associated data, you can submit a deletion request.

## How to request deletion:

To verify your identity and ensure the security of your data, please send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your ARrange account.

**1. Send an email to:** `[Insert Your Email Address Here]`
**2. Subject Line:** "Account Deletion Request"
**3. Body:** Please include the email address associated with your account and state that you wish for your account and all synced furniture/measurement data to be permanently deleted from our servers.

## What happens next?

Once we receive your request, we will process it within **7 business days**. 

*   Your Supabase authentication account will be permanently deleted.
*   All cloud-synced furniture records and spatial measurements will be wiped from our PostgreSQL database.
*   All uploaded images in your cloud storage bucket will be permanently deleted.

*Note: This action is irreversible. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Data stored locally on your device will remain until you uninstall the ARrange application.*
